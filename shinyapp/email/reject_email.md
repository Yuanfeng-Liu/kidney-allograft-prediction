#### 🏥 **Symptoms to Watch For**

If you experience any of the following, please contact your care team **immediately**

-   Fatigue, lethargy, feeling of illness, or lack of appetite

-   Flu-like symptoms (chills, body-aches, nausea, cough, shortness of breath)

-   Decrease in urine output Pain, swelling, or tenderness near the transplant site

-   Sudden weight gain or increased blood sugar

-   Decline in kidney function

#### 🛡️ **Staying Proactive**

Your lifestyle also plays a crucial role in maintaining transplant health

-   Maintain a balanced diet rich in protein and calcium

-   Avoid foods high in potassium, phosphate, or magnesium unless advised

-   Engage in light physical activity to support recovery

-   Always follow your transplant dietician’s advice
