#### 🧭 **Symptoms to Watch For**

Please still be alert for any unusual symptoms, and notify your transplant team if you notice any of the following

-   Fatigue, lethargy, feeling of illness, or lack of appetite

-   Flu-like symptoms *(chills, body-aches, nausea, cough, shortness of breath)*

-   Decrease in urine output

-   Pain, swelling, or tenderness near the transplant site

-   Sudden weight gain or increased blood sugar

-   Decline in kidney function
