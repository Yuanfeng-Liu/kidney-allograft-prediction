To support confident interpretation of results, the **Model Insights** section outlines the data, methods, and performance behind our prediction model.

This space is designed for clinicians and researchers who want transparency around **how predictions are made** - from the raw gene expression data to the final outputs shown in the Risk Prediction tab.

The section is divided into **4 parts**:

1.  **Training Datasets**: a summary of the source data used to build the model, including cohort sizes and preprocessing
2.  **Key Genes for Prediction**: a list of the most informative genes identified during feature selection, with links to further resources if available
3.  **Model Construction**: an explanation of the modeling pipeline, including batch correction, feature selection, algorithm choice, and configuration
4.  **Model Performance**: evaluation metrics used to assess how well the model generalises, including accuracy, AUC, sensitivity, and specificity

You can freely switch between **blood** and **biopsy** data modes using the toggle at the top right of this section. All visualisations and content will update accordingly, so you can compare how the model behaves across input types.

While review this information is optional, we strongly believe that understanding how the model works builds trust in its predictions and supports more informed clinical decisions.

We hope that through this deeper exploration, you’ll feel empowered to use the **Allograft Rejection Prediction** app as a reliable tool for preliminary risk assessment in your patients!
